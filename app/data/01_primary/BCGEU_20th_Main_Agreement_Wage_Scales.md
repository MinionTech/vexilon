# APPENDIX 3 - Classifications and Rates of Pay

## CORRECTIONAL AND SHERIFF SERVICES

Definition - This component includes those classifications performing duties related to providing secure
care and custody to adults in correctional centres, to youths in youth custody centres, and classifications
performing duties related to court security and the secure transport of persons who are under an order
of the Court.

## RETAIL STORES & WAREHOUSE

Definition - This component consists of those classifications directly involved in the operation of retail
stores and related warehouse operations.

## SOCIAL, INFORMATION & HEALTH

Definition - This component consists of those classifications involved in sociological, cultural, education,
research, health care delivery, and the direct technical support functions thereto.

## ADMINISTRATIVE PROFESSIONALS

Definition - This component consists of those classifications which act in direct support to administrative,
social and legal programs by providing such services as clerical, stenographic, business-machine
operation, etc., or which are of an administrative or supervisory nature which may include technical
support and auditing, taxation, systems, or regulatory/enforcement in a commercial environment.

## ENVIRONMENT, TECHNICAL AND OPERATIONS

Definition - This component consists of classifications involved in natural resources and environmental
protection and regulation; engineering support/inspection; labouring/maintenance; operation of vehicles
and industrial equipment; tradespersons, and marine crew and officers.

## Step 6

Effective April 1, 2028, the Employer will introduce a new step 6 for all grid levels in the agreement. The
value of step 6 will be the value of step 5 increased by 0.5%.
Upon the effective date of introduction to the new step 6, the following shall apply:

(1) Full-time employees who have been in step 5 for a minimum of 12 months will move to step 6.
All other full-time employees will move to step 6 the first day of the full pay period following their 12-
month anniversary in step 5.

(2) Part-time and auxiliary employees who have accumulated a minimum of 1827 hours in step 5
will move to step 6. All other part-time and auxiliary employees will move to step 6 the first day of the
pay period following the accumulation of 1827 hours in step 5.

(3) All substitutions, promotions, demotions, and reclassifications after the applicable effective
date, will utilize all active steps of Appendix 3A.

(4) Employees on a leave of absence without pay, maternity, adoption, parental, or educational
leave, that lasts more than 30 consecutive days will have their step 6 increment date deferred by the
period of the leave of absence.

continue to be employed as an auxiliary Warehouse worker R9. Auxiliary Warehouse workers R9
who are appointed to a regular Warehouse worker R9 position will be placed on the
grid (Appendix 3B) at the closest step not less than their current rate of pay.

the store system, will have their classification maintained at grid 13 so long as they occupy such a
